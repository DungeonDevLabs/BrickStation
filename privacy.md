# BrickStation Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · [Português](privacidade.md)

BrickStation is a launcher made by Dungeon Dev Labs. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it has no server of ours. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

## What stays on your phone
Your game list, favorites, ROM folder choice, theme, play time, paired PC (address, MAC and access token) and touch-control profiles are stored only on your device.

## Permissions and why
- **Accessibility service:** opens the quick menu over games, reads controller buttons and closes games. It does not read or store what you type or see.
- **Usage access:** play time per game, shown only to you.
- **Draw over other apps:** overlays on the TV.
- **Location / Wi-Fi state:** Android requires it to read the Wi-Fi network name shown in the system stats. It is not stored or sent.
- **Network, multicast:** find and talk to your own PC on your home network.
- **Secure settings (optional, granted by you over USB):** switch to the controller keyboard.
- **Kill background processes, delete packages:** close a game or uninstall it, always with Android's own confirmation.

## Your PC
If you use the PC streaming, the app talks directly to the BrickStation Link extension on your PC, inside your local network, using a token created when you paired. Video is carried by Sunshine and Moonlight, which have their own policies.

## Internet requests
The app may download public images (console and emulator icons) and open store pages for emulators in your browser. These servers see your IP address like any website.

## Children
BrickStation is not directed to children and collects no personal data.
